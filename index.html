Take Care of Your Jewellery

Simple general tips to help maintain the beauty of your jewellery pieces.

  • Keep jewellery away from harsh chemicals.

  • Store pieces separately to reduce scratching.

  • Keep jewellery dry when appropriate.

  • Clean pieces according to their material.

  • Ask a professional jeweller for care advice for valuable pieces.
